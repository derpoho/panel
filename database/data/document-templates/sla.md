## 1. What this covers

This agreement applies to workspaces on the **Business** plan, alongside our [terms of service](https://serveros.com/terms). It covers the ServerOS panel at serveros.com and your workspace’s address, and the connection the daemon uses to reach the panel.

It does not cover your servers, your hosting provider, or the apps running on them. Those stay under your control, and they keep running even if the panel is unavailable.

## 2. Our commitment

The panel will be available at least 99.9% of the time in each calendar month.

## 3. How uptime is measured

Monthly uptime is the share of minutes in the month when the panel was available. A minute counts as unavailable when our external monitoring finds the panel unreachable or returning server errors.

These don’t count as unavailable time:

- Scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours ahead
- Problems with your servers, your hosting provider, or the network between them and us
- Features marked as experimental, such as MCP
- Events outside our reasonable control, or suspension under our terms

## 4. Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, your workspace gets a credit against future invoices, as a share of that month’s Business fee:

| Monthly uptime | Credit |
| -------------- | ------ |
| Below 99.9%    | 10%    |
| Below 99.0%    | 25%    |
| Below 95.0%    | 50%    |

Credits are capped at 50% of the month’s fee, aren’t paid out as cash, and are the only remedy for missing this commitment.

## 5. Claiming a credit

Email [support@serveros.com](mailto:support@serveros.com) within 30 days of the end of the month, with your workspace and the times you saw the panel unavailable. We’ll check them against our monitoring and apply any credit to your next invoice.
